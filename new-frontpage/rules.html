GLOBAL RULES
  1. Do not upload, post, discuss, request, or link to anything that violates local laws or the laws of the United States.
  2. If you are under the age of 18, you must immediately leave this website and not continue accessing it.
  3. Do not create low-quality troll threads or posts, or intentionally disrupt discussions (e.g. starting flame wars or provoking users).
  4. Do not post, request, or distribute personal information ("doxxing"), nor organize or encourage raids against individuals or communities.
  5. Do not spam or flood the boards. If you accidentally double-post, please delete the duplicate or we will delete it instead. If you continue double-posting even after deletion you will be banned.
  6. Do not encourage self-harm, suicide, or any behavior that could reasonably put others at risk.
  7. Avoid starting or participating in discussions centered around morality-related discourse or past controversies which are likely to divide users. (e.g. Rabbit Hole, Zaako, etc.).
  8. Do not post gore, real-life pornography, or any graphic, grotesque, or sexually suggestive content involving real people.
  9. Do not sign your posts with a name, avatar, or signature as you would on a traditional forum.
  10. Advertising of any kind is prohibited, including Discord servers, social media accounts, and commercial services. Similarly, you may not promote or advertise 39chan on other websites without prior permission from both Team 39 and the owner of the website in question.
  11. Hate speech and divisive topics (including politics, religion, racism, and similar subjects) are not permitted. This is a hobby discussion website, not /pol/.
  12. Do not post low-effort imageboard spam (e.g. overused slang, pepes, jaks, etc.). Violations of this rule may result in a permanent, non-appealable ban and you risk having your entire device restricted from ever accessing this site again.
  13. Do not harass, target, or organize attacks against members of the Vocaloid community, including producers, artists, developers, or fans. Constructive criticism is welcome; personal attacks are not.
  14. This website is not centered around gossip or drama, so we ask that you refrain from creating threads or posts encouraging these types of discussions. What happens on Twitter stays on Twitter, unless it's news.
BOARD-SPECIFIC RULES
/leek/ - Vocaloid Lounge
  1. All threads and posts must relate to Vocaloid, Synthesizer V, UTAU, or other vocal synthesizer software.
  2. Character threads must contain meaningful discussion or content (music, news, artwork, original creations, etc.). Image dump threads should remain on /c/.
  3. Global Rules 3, 7, 13 and 14 will be strictly enforced.
  4. Pornographic content (including illustrations) is strictly prohibited under this board. Discussion of sexual topics and the posting of non-explicit ''ecchi'' images is permitted (so long as it is tagged as NSFW).
/negi/ - Vocaloid Lounge (JP)
  1. The same rules that apply on /leek/ apply here.
/mmd/ - MikuMikuDance
  1. This board is dedicated to MikuMikuDance and related 3D creation software, including Blender, Maya, Source Filmmaker, Koikatsu, and similar tools. Keep all threads and posts on topic.
  2. Any posts related to MikuMikuOnline should be kept within its respective discussion thread.
/live/ - Vocaloid Live Events
  1. Global Rules 1, 4 and 8 will be strictly enforced.
  2. Do not harass or make any type of sexual, violent or discrminative comments towards any users posting self photographs (selfies) on this board.
/o/ - Art / Oekaki
  1. AI-generated artwork is not permitted on this board, unless it is provided exclusively as a reference for artists in request threads.
  2. NSFW/R-18 artwork must be tagged properly. Unlike on other boards, this rule does not apply to suggestive ''ecchi'' works.
/c/ - Vocaloid/Cute
  1. Images should be cute ("moe") in nature.
  2. Hentai artwork is strictly prohibited on this board. Ecchi is allowed, but we suggest that you tag it as NSFW.
  3. Do not upload low quality AI-generated artwork.
  4. Do not dump real-life images. This includes cosplay photos.
/j/ - Japanese Culture
  1. Global rule 11 will be strictly enforced. You shall not discuss politics even in a historical context.
/meta/ - Site Discussion
  1. You may only discuss issues related to the 39chan website & software on this board.
